Screens

Three windows, one surface.

The redesign keeps the real geometry and upgrades the shell: a step-by-step onboarding with BYOK, a breathing bar, and a chatbox that grows out of it — keyed to a deep slate accent (#3a7294) on primary actions and live states.

Floating window + chatbox

A 112×47 capsule at rest — iris, screenshot, mic — breathing over the desktop. Type anywhere and it stretches into the 353×47 input bar; ⏎ sends and a chatbox extends with the streamed answer. Docks on all four edges, light and dark, plus the lock and permission gates.
