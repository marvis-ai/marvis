1 / 5 · welcome

Your screen, answered.

Marvis is a small bar that floats over your work. It sees what you see, hears the call you're in, and streams an answer without switching windows.

No account. Nothing signs in, syncs, or phones home.
Your own keys. OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini — or Ollama, fully local.
Invisible to share. The overlay never appears in screenshots or screen share.

A vault only you can open.

Provider keys are sealed into keys.enc with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key lives in your Mac's Keychain and only releases behind Touch ID or your password — never a passphrase to forget.

Let Marvis see your screen.

Answers attach the latest frame — a four-per-second, deduplicated ring that holds the last 60 seconds and never writes to disk. macOS will ask once for Screen Recording.

Bring your own key.

Pick a provider and paste a key. Marvis validates it against the API before storing — a bad key is an answer, not a crash.

Calls api.openai.com once to check the key, then seals it into keys.enc.

That key didn't validate — check it and try again.
