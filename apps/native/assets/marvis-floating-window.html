The bar breathes. The chatbox grows out of it.

At rest Marvis is a 112 px capsule — the iris, a screenshot-ask, the mic — idling on a slow seven-second breath. Click the iris, or just start typing, and it stretches into the 353 px input bar: the floating window's input status, the iris folding into a back arrow as it grows. ⏎ sends and the chatbox extends below with a streamed answer while the bar keeps the field for follow-ups; the arrow or Esc settles it back to the capsule. The camera screenshots straight into an ask; the mic turns the same surface into a listener. It docks on any screen edge — the bar top and bottom, a slim rail left and right where the composer moves into the chatbox — and follows the system into dark mode. Try it: just start typing.
